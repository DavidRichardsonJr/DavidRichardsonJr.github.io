Self-drawn coordinate grid. Always works, no internet needed.

What image works: any JPG/PNG/scan of your park's trail map, ideally north-up (not rotated) and not artistically distorted. A straight photo or PDF export of an official trail map works well; a stylized "not to scale" tourist illustration will place posts less accurately the further they are from your 2 reference points.

Click "Pick", then click that spot on the map image on the right. Then give that exact spot's real-world coordinates — either pick one of your already-loaded posts below (auto-fills its known lat/lon), or type coordinates manually. Do this for two points that are far apart (e.g. opposite ends of the trail).

Color-codes markers by the "Action"/"Status" column in your CSV, for maintenance planning. Not for visitor-facing use — this is a convenience toggle, not real access control; anyone with this file can turn it on. Genuine staff-only restriction would need a login-gated deployment.
